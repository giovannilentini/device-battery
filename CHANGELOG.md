# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Two devices of the same model (e.g. two mice, each on its own receiver) are told apart by
  their serial number: one disconnecting is noticed while the other stays, and each can be
  hidden or renamed on its own. Settings made before keep applying
- Low battery notifications follow every widget instance: they are sent if any instance has
  them on, at the highest *Low battery at* level among those
- Screen readers read the devices in the popup and on the desktop
- The widget can be translated (see *Translating* in the README)
- *About* links to the project page and its issues

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-07)

- AirPods: in-ear detection, noise control mode, model name, low battery notifications
- Recently disconnected devices with their last levels
- Hide and rename devices, per widget instance
- Bluetooth settings and forget actions in the context menu
- Adaptive grid of rings on the desktop
- Icons for every UPower device type
- Connected devices without battery information are named in the popup
- Fixes: empty popup message, panel popup always shows the list
